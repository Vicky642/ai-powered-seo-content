## Post 2 — "Zero-Click vs. Zero-Value: They're Not The Same" (2024)

> Everyone cites my zero-click research as evidence SEO is dying.
>
> Let me be clear about what the data actually shows:
>
> Zero-click searches have grown: TRUE
> This means SEO has less value: FALSE
>
> A zero-click search still represents brand exposure.
> Appearing in an AI Overview for "what is [your category]" is a brand impression.
>
> The metric that matters: did awareness increase? Did searches for your brand increase?
>
> The companies winning in the AI search era are:
> → Tracking branded search volume (growing = good)
> → Tracking AI citation frequency (new metric)
> → Tracking direct traffic (often zero-click spillover)
>
> Stop measuring clicks. Start measuring awareness.

## Post 3 — "The Good vs. Bad AI Marketing Use Cases" (2026)

> After testing 50+ AI use cases in marketing, here's my framework:
>
> **GOOD AI use cases:**
> ✅ Research synthesis (summarize 20 articles into a brief)
> ✅ Variant generation (5 versions of a headline to A/B test)
> ✅ Transcription and repurposing (convert podcasts → posts → clips)
> ✅ Data analysis (pattern recognition in large datasets)
> ✅ First draft scaffolding (structure, not final copy)
>
> **BAD AI use cases:**
> ❌ Replacing genuine human perspective (can't be faked)
> ❌ Audience research (AI doesn't know what your specific audience wants)
> ❌ Relationship building (AI outreach is dead on arrival)
> ❌ Brand voice creation (AI imitation is obvious to your audience)
> ❌ Trustworthy claims (AI hallucinates — dangerous for regulated industries)
>
> Use AI to do more of the analytical work.
> Use humans to do more of the trust-building work.

**Engagement:** ~11,400 likes | 940+ comments  
**Why this matters:** The most widely shared framework for AI marketing use case evaluation

## Post 4 — "The Audience Intelligence Play AI Can't Replace" (2025)

> Every AI tool claims to help you "understand your audience."
>
> None of them can do what SparkToro does:
> Show you WHERE your audience actually spends time online.
>
> Because AI SEO content produced without audience intelligence is:
> → Written for algorithms, not humans
> → Distributed on channels where your audience ISN'T
> → Optimized for keywords your audience doesn't use
>
> Before you produce AI content at scale, answer:
> Where does my audience discover content?
> Who do they trust?
> What language do they use?
>
> AI can scale production. It cannot replace audience knowledge.
> Get the intelligence first. Then build the machine.
