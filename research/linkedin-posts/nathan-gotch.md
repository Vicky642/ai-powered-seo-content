## Post 3 — "Systemize or Suffer" (2024)

> The biggest SEO mistake I see: treating SEO like a series of tactics.
>
> Win → duplicate tactic → wonder why it doesn't work again.
>
> The fix: build systems, not tactics.
>
> A system for AI content production looks like:
>
> 1. Keyword research SOP (weekly, with AI assistance)
> 2. Content brief template (NLP-optimized, AI-generated)
> 3. Writing workflow (AI draft → human experience layer)
> 4. Publishing checklist (schema, internal links, images)
> 5. Monitoring schedule (monthly relevance decay check)
> 6. Update protocol (when and how to refresh old content)
>
> When you have a system, you don't need to make decisions.
> You just execute.

## Post 4 — "Compact Keywords Are Underrated" (2025)

> Everyone chases high-volume keywords.
>
> I chase "compact keywords." Here's why:
>
> Compact keywords have:
> → Clear, specific intent (commercial or transactional)
> → Low competition (because most SEOs ignore them)
> → High conversion rate (because they're specific)
>
> Example:
> "SEO tools" = broad, competitive, ambiguous intent
> "SEO tool for local service businesses under $100/month" = compact, specific, ready to buy
>
> AI makes it trivially easy to produce content for compact keywords at scale.
> The research to find GOOD compact keywords is still human work.
>
> That's your unfair advantage.

**Engagement:** ~2,300 likes | 170+ comments  
**Why this matters:** Specific keyword strategy that works WITH AI content production at scale

## Post 5 — "The On-Page SEO Checklist That Still Works" (2024)

> In 2024, this on-page SEO foundation still drives rankings:
>
> ✅ Target keyword in H1 (naturally)
> ✅ Related keywords in H2s (NLP signals)
> ✅ Target keyword in first 100 words
> ✅ Internal links to/from topic cluster pages
> ✅ Descriptive alt text on all images
> ✅ Schema markup (Article, FAQ, HowTo as relevant)
> ✅ Page experience: CLS < 0.1, LCP < 2.5s, INP < 200ms
> ✅ Content comprehensiveness score ≥ 70 (Rankability)
>
> AI can handle 70% of this automatically.
> The 30% that needs human input: experience signals, unique data, brand voice.
>
> Don't let AI shortcuts cut out the 30% that matters.

**Why this matters:** Concrete on-page checklist showing where AI helps and where humans must lead
