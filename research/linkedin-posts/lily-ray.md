## Post 3 — "E-E-A-T Is Not a Checklist" (2024)

> The most dangerous misconception in SEO right now:
>
> "I added an author bio with credentials, so I have E-E-A-T."
>
> E-E-A-T is not a checklist. It's a holistic signal.
>
> Google evaluates:
> → Is this author known across the web for this topic?
> → Are other authoritative sources referencing this content?
> → Does the page demonstrate ACTUAL experience (not just claimed expertise)?
>
> AI can write an author bio. AI cannot fake a decade of industry presence.
>
> The most sustainable AI content strategy builds real author authority first.
> Then uses AI to scale what's already trusted.

**Engagement:** ~3,700 likes | 260+ comments  
**Why this matters:** Defines the right foundation for AI content production in a post-Helpful Content world

## Post 5 — "Too Much SEO Kills Rankings" (2025)

> Some sites are losing rankings not because of bad SEO — but because of too much SEO.
>
> Signs you've over-optimized:
>
> ❌ Keyword stuffed title tags ("Best SEO Tool | Top SEO Software | SEO Platform 2024")
> ❌ Exact-match anchors on every internal link
> ❌ AI-generated FAQ sections that don't answer real questions
> ❌ Structured data markup on every element of every page
>
> Google is getting better at identifying sites that are "gaming" vs. "informing."
>
> The winning formula in 2025:
> Create content for humans. Use SEO to make it findable.
> Not the other way around.
