## Post 3 — "How LLMs Decide What to Include in Answers" (2025)

> I've spent months studying how LLMs select content for answers.
>
> Here's what the pattern shows:
>
> 1. **Source authority**: Is this domain trusted across the web? (not just DR)
> 2. **Topical co-occurrence**: Do authoritative sources link TO this + discuss this TOPIC?
> 3. **Freshness**: For evolving topics, recency is heavily weighted
> 4. **Structural clarity**: Content that's easy to parse gets cited more
> 5. **Entity alignment**: Does this content's entity structure match the query's entity structure?
>
> The #1 factor people ignore: structural clarity.
>
> If your content requires a human to parse — it requires an LLM to parse.
> LLMs choose the easy path. Structure your content accordingly.

**Engagement:** ~5,100 likes | 410+ comments  
**Why this matters:** Original research on LLM citation behavior — directly actionable for content production
