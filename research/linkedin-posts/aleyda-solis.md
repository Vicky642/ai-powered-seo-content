## Post 1 — "AI Mode Changes Everything About Content Structure" (2025)

> Google's AI Mode uses a "Query Fan-Out" technique.
>
> Here's what this means for your content strategy:
>
> When you search, AI Mode doesn't run ONE query.
> It runs MULTIPLE sub-queries simultaneously to gather information from different angles.
>
> Implications for AI SEO content production:
>
> ✅ One page needs to answer multiple related sub-questions
> ✅ FAQ-style sections become more important than ever
> ✅ Semantic richness > keyword density
> ✅ Content that anticipates follow-up questions will be cited more
>
> If your content only answers the main query, you're leaving AI Mode citations on the table.

## Post 2 — "My AI Content Workflow for Clients" (2024)

> After running AI-assisted SEO for 20+ enterprise clients, here's the workflow that actually works:
>
> **Phase 1: Research (80% human, 20% AI)**
> - Identify the actual search intent
> - Map the competitive landscape
> - Find content gaps with real data
>
> **Phase 2: Creation (50/50)**
> - AI drafts the structure
> - Human adds experience, data, perspective
>
> **Phase 3: Optimization (70% AI, 30% human)**
> - AI handles internal linking suggestions
> - AI handles schema markup
> - Human validates everything
>
> **Phase 4: Distribution (90% AI, 10% human)**
> - AI reformats for different channels
> - Human approves and publishes
>
> The companies failing with AI are either all-AI or no-AI. Balance wins.

**Engagement:** ~4,100 likes | 340+ comments  
**Why this matters:** Concrete workflow with specific human/AI ratios at each stage

## Post 5 — "International AI SEO Has Unique Challenges" (2025)

> Running international SEO campaigns with AI content?
>
> Here are the challenges nobody talks about:
>
> 🌐 LLMs have unequal training data by language
> → AI-generated content in Spanish or Portuguese may have lower factual accuracy
>
> 🌐 AI Overviews rollout is uneven by country
> → US-focused AI SEO advice doesn't translate directly
>
> 🌐 Entity recognition varies by market
> → Your brand may not exist as a known entity in multilingual LLM training
>
> International AI content production needs localized quality review — not just translation.
>
> The AI content tools are mostly built for the US market. Know that going in.
