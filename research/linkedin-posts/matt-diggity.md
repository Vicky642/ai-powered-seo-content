## Post 2 — "The AI Content Workflow That's Working in 2024" (2024)

> Here's the exact workflow we use for AI content that ranks:
>
> 1. **Research** (human): Keyword + SERP analysis. What's the real intent?
> 2. **Outline** (AI + human review): Generate structure, validate against top 10 results
> 3. **Draft** (AI): Write the full draft to the outline
> 4. **Experience Layer** (human): Add real examples, data, opinions — things AI can't fake
> 5. **Fact-check** (human): Verify every claim. AI hallucinates.
> 6. **SEO Layer** (AI-assisted): Internal links, title tag variants, meta description
> 7. **Publish + Monitor**: Track with rank trackers, update quarterly
>
> Steps 4 and 5 are where 90% of people skip. Don't skip them.

**Engagement:** ~5,400 likes | 390+ comments  
**Why this matters:** Specific, actionable AI content production SOP that's been battle-tested
