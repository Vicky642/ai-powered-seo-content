## Selection Rationale

These 10 experts were chosen because each satisfies at least **3 of 4** criteria:

| Criteria | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Practitioner** | Runs real campaigns, clients, or tools — not just a writer |
| **Original data** | Publishes research, experiments, or proprietary data |
| **Signal density** | High ratio of insight to noise in their content |
| **AI-SEO specificity** | Directly addresses AI-powered content production workflows |

Deliberately excluded: generic "top 50 SEO influencers" lists, PR consultants who write about SEO but don't practice it, and purely journalistic voices.
